Room-by-room LiDAR scans of my family's shore house in New Jersey, captured on an iPhone in the summer of 2025. They are the reference for a clean Blender rebuild of the house that is still in progress: each room is traced over its scan, then baked to its own texture. The scans themselves are raw, seams and all, which is part of what I like about them.

Click a room to load it, then drag to orbit and scroll or pinch to zoom. Each scan was cut down for the browser with gltf-transform (welded, simplified, quantized, its 8K texture re-encoded to a 1024 px WebP), so a room is a few hundred kilobytes to a megabyte and nothing loads until you ask for it.
